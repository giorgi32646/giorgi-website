# Brag Plan: Giorgi Lortkipanidze — Designer & Developer

## What is this app?
A portfolio + services site for a one-person designer-developer who designs SaaS interfaces in Figma and ships them as production React — "no handoff, no delays."

## The angle
Announce a solo freelancer like a 2016 unicorn. Press-release dateline, "We're thrilled to announce our Series A", a pitch-deck slide calling it "the Uber for design handoff", a Traction slide, and a closing "now raising" line — except the round is a client project and the headcount is 1. Played completely straight. Every real claim and number is pulled from the site; only the 2016-launch framing is parody.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Press-release card on the site's navy (#0A0E1A): a small dateline "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — 2016" and a line typed out character by character:
"Today, we're thrilled to announce our Series A."
Then a small mono stamp lands underneath: "Team size: 1".

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real hero, rebuilt: pulsing "Available for freelance work" badge → "Giorgi Lortkipanidze" slams in (Syne 800) → "Designer & Developer" in the white→blue gradient → subline "I design and build production-ready SaaS interfaces — no handoff, no delays."
- Pitch slide "3 / 12": "We're the Uber for design handoff." — "handoff" gets struck through in blue, and the three real Why cards deal in: No Handoff Delays / Async-First Communication / Production-Ready Code.
- Traction slide: the About section's stats count up — 6+ Years of experience, 50+ Products shipped, 100% Remote-native — over a deadpan hockey-stick line.
- Social proof as a press quote: "Giorgi delivered our entire design system in three weeks." — CTO, RedFlag.ai

## Outro / punchline
"Now raising:" → "one Full MVP. $15–30k." (the real pricing tier). Then the lockup: Giorgi Lortkipanidze / Designer & Developer / giorgilortkipanidze.online, with a tiny mono footer "Headcount: 1 · Handoffs: 0".

## User flow worth showing
Portfolio / services site — no product app. The "flow" is the hiring path the site itself sells: see the hero → understand the no-handoff pitch → see proof (stats, testimonial) → pick a pricing tier. The storyboard follows that path, framed as a launch deck.
